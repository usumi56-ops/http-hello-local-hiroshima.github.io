Nice to meet you.

This project starts with one simple question: What do travelers really want to know?

🌿

I'm Sumire.

A 15-year-old student living in Hiroshima, Japan, and the person behind Hello, Local Hiroshima.

In the future, I want to become a bridge connecting Japan with people around the world. While thinking about what I can do right now, I came up with this project.

But I don't want to decide what travelers need without asking them first. That's why this website is starting with your stories, questions and experiences.

The goal is to learn from travelers and use their feedback to create useful information about the local side of Hiroshima.

What are we looking for?

We are currently researching what makes exploring local Japan exciting, confusing, or difficult for travelers.
